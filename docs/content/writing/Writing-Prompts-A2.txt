PELA Writing Prompts - Level A2
(25 prompts - for Admin upload test, UC-19 Writing import flow)

1. [Travel - paragraph] Write about a trip you took last year. Where did you go, and what did you do there? (~40-70 words)

2. [Work - email] Write an email to your manager asking for a day off next week. Explain your reason. (~40-70 words)

3. [Daily Life - paragraph] Describe a typical weekend for you. What activities do you usually do? (~40-70 words)

4. [Technology - paragraph] Write about a time your phone or computer stopped working. What happened, and how did you fix it? (~40-70 words)

5. [Work - paragraph] Describe your first day at a new job or school. How did you feel? (~40-70 words)

6. [Daily Life - email] Write an email to a friend describing your new apartment or house. (~40-70 words)

7. [Travel - email] Write an email to a hotel asking about their room prices and available dates. (~40-70 words)

8. [Technology - paragraph] Write about an app or website you use every day. How does it help you? (~40-70 words)

9. [Daily Life - paragraph] Describe your best friend. What do they look like, and what do you like to do together? (~40-70 words)

10. [Work - email] Write an email to a colleague asking them to cover your shift next Monday. (~40-70 words)

11. [Daily Life - paragraph] Describe the last time you went shopping. What did you buy, and why? (~40-70 words)

12. [Daily Life - paragraph] Write about a time you were sick. What happened, and what did you do to get better? (~40-70 words)

13. [Daily Life - email] Write an email to a friend inviting them to watch a sports match with you this weekend. (~40-70 words)

14. [Daily Life - paragraph] Describe a hobby you started recently. Why did you choose it, and how often do you do it? (~40-70 words)

15. [Work - email] Write an email to your teacher asking for extra help with a subject you find difficult. (~40-70 words)

16. [Daily Life - paragraph] Write about one thing you do to help protect the environment. (~40-70 words)

17. [Daily Life - paragraph] Describe a festival or celebration in your country. What do people do? (~40-70 words)

18. [Daily Life - email] Write an email to a friend inviting them to cook dinner together at your place. (~40-70 words)

19. [Travel - paragraph] Describe a place you would like to visit someday and explain why. (~40-70 words)

20. [Work - paragraph] Write about a skill you learned at work or school recently. (~40-70 words)

21. [Travel - paragraph] Describe a trip you took with your family or friends. What was the best part? (~40-70 words)

22. [Travel - email] Write an email to a friend asking for travel tips for a city they have visited. (~40-70 words)

23. [Technology - email] Write an email to a shop asking for help with a product that stopped working. (~40-70 words)

24. [Technology - paragraph] Write about how technology helps you study or do your job. (~40-70 words)

25. [Technology - paragraph] Describe a new gadget you recently bought. What does it do? (~40-70 words)
