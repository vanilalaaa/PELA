PELA Writing Prompts - Level B1
(25 prompts - for Admin upload test, UC-19 Writing import flow)

1. [Travel - essay] Some people prefer traveling alone, while others prefer traveling in groups. Which do you prefer, and why? (~80-120 words)

2. [Work - paragraph] Describe a job you would like to have in the future. What skills do you need for it? (~80-120 words)

3. [Daily Life - essay] Do you think it's important to have a daily routine? Explain your opinion. (~80-120 words)

4. [Technology - essay] Do you think smartphones make our lives easier or more complicated? Give your opinion with reasons. (~80-120 words)

5. [Work - email] Write an email to your boss explaining a problem you had with a project and how you solved it. (~80-120 words)

6. [Travel - paragraph] Describe your dream vacation. Where would you go, and what would you do? (~80-120 words)

7. [Daily Life - paragraph] Describe a memorable celebration or event from your life (e.g., a wedding, a holiday). (~80-120 words)

8. [Technology - paragraph] Describe how technology has changed the way people communicate with their families. (~80-120 words)

9. [Work - essay] Do you think it's better to work for a big company or a small company? Explain your choice. (~80-120 words)

10. [Daily Life - essay] Some people think social media brings people closer together, while others think it makes them more isolated. What do you think? (~80-120 words)

11. [Daily Life - essay] Do you think fast food is bad for people's health? Give your opinion with reasons. (~80-120 words)

12. [Daily Life - paragraph] Describe one environmental problem in your city and suggest a simple solution. (~80-120 words)

13. [Daily Life - essay] Do you think it's important for young people to volunteer in their community? Explain your opinion. (~80-120 words)

14. [Work - paragraph] Describe a teacher who influenced you. What did you learn from them? (~80-120 words)

15. [Daily Life - essay] Do you think online shopping is better than shopping in a store? Give your opinion. (~80-120 words)

16. [Daily Life - paragraph] Describe a sport you think everyone should try at least once, and explain why. (~80-120 words)

17. [Daily Life - paragraph] Describe a tradition from your country that you think is important to keep. (~80-120 words)

18. [Work - email] Write an email to a potential employer asking about an internship opportunity at their company. (~80-120 words)

19. [Daily Life - essay] Do you think it's better to live in a big city or a small town? Explain your choice. (~80-120 words)

20. [Technology - paragraph] Describe how you use technology to help you study or learn new things. (~80-120 words)

21. [Travel - essay] Do you think it's better to plan a trip in detail or travel spontaneously? Explain your opinion. (~80-120 words)

22. [Travel - paragraph] Describe the most interesting place you have ever visited and why it stood out to you. (~80-120 words)

23. [Travel - email] Write an email to a travel agency asking about group tour packages for a family trip. (~80-120 words)

24. [Technology - essay] Do you think people spend too much time on their phones? Give your opinion with reasons. (~80-120 words)

25. [Technology - paragraph] Describe an app or website that changed the way you do something in daily life. (~80-120 words)
