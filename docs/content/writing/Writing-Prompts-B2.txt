PELA Writing Prompts - Level B2
(20 prompts - for Admin upload test, UC-19 Writing import flow)

1. [Technology - essay] Some people believe social media does more harm than good. Discuss both sides and give your opinion. (~120-180 words)

2. [Work - essay] Many companies now allow employees to work from home. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? Discuss. (~120-180 words)

3. [Society - essay] Some people think students should choose their own subjects at school, while others think the curriculum should be fixed. Discuss both views and give your opinion. (~120-180 words)

4. [Environment - essay] What can individuals do to help reduce plastic waste in their daily lives? Discuss with examples. (~120-180 words)

5. [Society - essay] Tourism can bring both benefits and problems to a city. Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of tourism. (~120-180 words)

6. [Work - email] Write a formal email to a company requesting information about a job vacancy you saw advertised. (~120-180 words)

7. [Environment - essay] Should governments offer subsidies to encourage households to switch to renewable energy sources? Discuss. (~120-180 words)

8. [Technology - paragraph] Describe how artificial intelligence might change the way people work in the next ten years. (~120-180 words)

9. [Society - essay] Some people think cities should ban cars from the city center. Do you agree or disagree? Give reasons. (~120-180 words)

10. [Work - email] Write a formal email to your manager requesting funding to attend a professional training course, explaining how it would benefit your team. (~120-180 words)

11. [Society - essay] Some people believe advertising aimed at children should be banned. Discuss both sides and give your opinion. (~120-180 words)

12. [Society - essay] Does globalization help or harm local cultures? Discuss with examples. (~120-180 words)

13. [Environment - essay] Some people think zoos should be closed because wild animals belong in their natural habitats. Discuss both views and give your opinion. (~120-180 words)

14. [Technology - essay] Should media literacy be taught as a required school subject to help students critically evaluate the information they encounter? Discuss. (~120-180 words)

15. [Environment - essay] Many people now buy second-hand clothes and furniture to protect the environment. Is this trend likely to make a real difference? Discuss. (~120-180 words)

16. [Work - essay] Should companies be required to offer a four-day work week without reducing employees' pay? Discuss the advantages and disadvantages. (~120-180 words)

17. [Work - email] Write a formal email to HR requesting a flexible working schedule and explaining your reasons. (~120-180 words)

18. [Technology - paragraph] Describe a scientific discovery or invention you think has changed the world the most. (~120-180 words)

19. [Technology - essay] Should autonomous vehicles be allowed on public roads before the technology is fully proven safe? Discuss. (~120-180 words)

20. [Environment - essay] Should people be encouraged to eat less meat in order to reduce their impact on the environment? Discuss both sides and give your opinion. (~120-180 words)
