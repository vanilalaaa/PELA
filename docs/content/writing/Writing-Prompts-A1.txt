PELA Writing Prompts - Level A1
(28 prompts - for Admin upload test, UC-19 Writing import flow)

1. [Travel - email] Write an email to a friend. Tell them where you want to go on your next trip and why. (~20-40 words)

2. [Daily Life - paragraph] Write about your daily routine. What time do you wake up, eat breakfast, and go to school or work? (~20-40 words)

3. [Work - email] Write an email to your teacher. Tell them you will be absent tomorrow and why. (~20-40 words)

4. [Technology - paragraph] Write about your favorite app. What is it, and what do you use it for? (~20-40 words)

5. [Daily Life - paragraph] Write about your family. How many people are in your family, and what are they like? (~20-40 words)

6. [Travel - paragraph] Write about a place you like to visit in your city. (~20-40 words)

7. [Daily Life - email] Write an email inviting a friend to your birthday party. (~20-40 words)

8. [Work - paragraph] Write about your job or your school. What do you do there every day? (~20-40 words)

9. [Technology - email] Write an email to a friend. Ask them to help you fix your phone. (~20-40 words)

10. [Daily Life - paragraph] What food do you enjoy eating most, and why do you like it? (~20-40 words)

11. [Daily Life - paragraph] Describe a shop you often visit. What do you usually buy there? (~20-40 words)

12. [Daily Life - paragraph] Write about what you do to stay healthy. Do you exercise or eat healthy food? (~20-40 words)

13. [Daily Life - email] Write an email to a friend. Invite them to play your favorite sport with you. (~20-40 words)

14. [Daily Life - paragraph] Write about your hobby. What do you do, and when do you do it? (~20-40 words)

15. [Daily Life - paragraph] Write about the weather in your city this week. (~20-40 words)

16. [Work - email] Write an email to a classmate. Ask them for help with your homework. (~20-40 words)

17. [Daily Life - paragraph] Write about your pet or an animal you like. (~20-40 words)

18. [Daily Life - paragraph] Which season do you enjoy the most, and what makes it special to you? (~20-40 words)

19. [Daily Life - email] Write an email to a shop. Ask what time they open and close. (~20-40 words)

20. [Daily Life - paragraph] Write about your best friend. What do you do together? (~20-40 words)

21. [Travel - paragraph] Write about your last trip to another city. What did you see there? (~20-40 words)

22. [Travel - email] Write an email to a friend. Tell them about a fun trip you took last month. (~20-40 words)

23. [Travel - paragraph] Write about how you usually travel — by bus, by car, or by plane. (~20-40 words)

24. [Work - paragraph] Write about a task you do every day at work or school. (~20-40 words)

25. [Work - email] Write an email to a coworker. Ask them to help you finish a task today. (~20-40 words)

26. [Technology - email] Write an email to a friend. Recommend an app you think they would like. (~20-40 words)

27. [Technology - paragraph] Write about how you use your phone every day. (~20-40 words)

28. [Technology - paragraph] Write about a gadget you want to buy. Why do you want it? (~20-40 words)
